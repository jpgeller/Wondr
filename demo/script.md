# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Before going on stage:
- Signed in on https://wondr-1isf.vercel.app, profile filled in, Google Calendar open in a second tab.
- One plan already generated earlier today (backup if the live run is slow).
- Backup video ready on the laptop.

## 0:00 The hook (20 s)

"Planning a free afternoon takes longer than the afternoon. WONDR flips it: you tell it when you're free, it plans a real outing, puts it on your calendar, and keeps it a mystery until you need to go."

## 0:20 Sign in and profile (20 s)

- Show the signed-in home: "One Google sign-in. It also grants calendar access."
- Show the profile: area, free window, interests.

## 0:40 Plan my day, live (60 s)

- Tap **Generate plan**.
- Read the progress messages out loud as they appear: "It's searching events, now it's opening the venue pages to check hours and addresses."
- "This is an agent on Omnara. It calls real tools: Exa search, Google Places, and it reads each venue's own website. Every stop must have a source and fit my window, or our code rejects it."
- If it takes too long, switch to the plan generated earlier.

## 1:40 The mystery (30 s)

- Show the plan: teaser and dress code, stops as locked cards with only a time.
- Switch to the Google Calendar tab: "WONDR · Mystery stop 1, 2, 3", already there, with the dress code.

## 2:10 The reveal (40 s)

- Turn on demo mode (or reveal a stop).
- The card unlocks: venue, address, directions, what to do.
- Back to Google Calendar, refresh: the event now has the real place and address.

## 2:50 Close (10 s)

"The agent did real research on the real web, wrote to a real calendar, and it's live right now at wondr-1isf.vercel.app."
